CS301 Operating Systems - End Semester Examination 2022
Time: 3 hours, Max Marks: 100

Q1. Explain Banker's Algorithm for deadlock avoidance with a suitable example.
    Given the following allocation and max matrices, determine if the system
    is in a safe state and find a safe sequence. [15 marks]

Q2. A disk queue has requests on cylinders: 98, 183, 37, 122, 14, 124, 65, 67.
    The disk head is at cylinder 53. Calculate total head movement using
    SCAN and C-SCAN disk scheduling algorithms. [15 marks]

Q3. What are the four necessary conditions for deadlock to occur? Explain
    each with an example. How does deadlock prevention differ from deadlock
    avoidance? [10 marks]

Q4. Differentiate between paging and segmentation. [8 marks]

Q5. Explain the producer-consumer problem and provide a solution using
    semaphores. Write pseudocode for wait() and signal() operations. [12 marks]

Q6. Compare FCFS, SJF and Round Robin scheduling with a Gantt chart example
    for 4 processes. [10 marks]

Q7. Explain the Readers-Writers problem and give a semaphore-based solution
    that avoids starvation. [10 marks]

Q8. What is virtual memory? Explain demand paging and page replacement
    algorithms (FIFO, LRU, Optimal) with an example reference string. [10 marks]
