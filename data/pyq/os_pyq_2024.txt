CS301 Operating Systems - End Semester Examination 2024
Time: 3 hours, Max Marks: 100

Q1. Explain deadlock avoidance using Banker's Algorithm. Given a system
    with 5 processes and 3 resource types, determine the safe sequence. [15 marks]

Q2. Given a sequence of disk requests, compute total head movement using
    SCAN, C-SCAN and C-LOOK disk scheduling algorithms. Compare their
    performance. [15 marks]

Q3. What is a race condition? Explain how semaphores prevent race
    conditions in the critical section problem with pseudocode. [10 marks]

Q4. Explain the Bounded Buffer (Producer-Consumer) problem and its
    semaphore-based solution in detail. [10 marks]

Q5. Differentiate deadlock prevention, avoidance and detection with
    real-world analogies. [10 marks]

Q6. Explain paging with a diagram showing logical to physical address
    translation using page tables and TLB. [10 marks]

Q7. What is starvation and aging in CPU scheduling? How does the Round
    Robin algorithm's time quantum affect performance? [8 marks]

Q8. Explain file allocation methods: contiguous, linked and indexed
    allocation with their advantages and disadvantages. [10 marks]
